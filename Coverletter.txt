
Dear Prof. Rigden, dear Editors,

Please find attached our manuscript describing the  new version of the tRNAdb 2009 by Jühling et al. for the NAR 2027 database issue. The tRNAdb 2009 has ammassed over a 1000 citations since its publication, but had to be taken off-line for security reasons in 2024.

We provide a description of the major technical overhaul of the database and its front-end. Furthermore, we describe the procedure we used to add new data to the tRNAdb. Lastly, we provide some statistics of the content of the database.
The tRNAdb was deployed again in early 2026, and now includes a redesigned website, and an API for automatic data retrieval.
Besides technical updates, we collected and curated approximately 180k nuclear tRNA sequences as well as 300k mitochondrial tRNA sequences and made them available together with the original dataset.

The number of tRNA sequences currently available is an increase by about a factor of 10 compared to the original tRNAdb 2009. Furthermore, the tRNAdb now includes tRNAs of previously underrepresented types, such as mitochondrial tRNAs from plants and fungi,  genomic tRNAs from major archaeal superphyla like Asgard, DPANN and TACK, and modified mitochondrial tRNAs.

We are therefore convinced that this update of the 2009 database paper "tRNAdb 2009: compilation of tRNA sequences and tRNA genes" is a useful contribution to the databse issue of NAR.
The updated tRNAdb is reachable at https://tdb.bioinf.uni-leipzig.de/.
