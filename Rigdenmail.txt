Suitability of tRNAdb 2026

Dear Prof. Rigden,

We write to inquire about the suitability of a new version of the tRNAdb 2009 by Jühling et al. for the NAR 2027 database issue. The tRNAdb 2009 has ammassed over a 1000 citations since its publication, but had to be taken off-line for security reasons in 2024.

After a major technical overhaul we were able deploy the tRNAdb again in early 2026 together with a redesigned website, and an API for automatic data retrieval. Besides technical updates, we collected and curated a large number of tRNA sequences and made them available together with the original dataset. 

The number of tRNA sequences currently available is an increase by about a factor of 10 compared to the original tRNAdb 2009. We are therefore convinced that an update of the 2009 database paper "tRNAdb 2009: compilation of tRNA sequences and tRNA genes" is indicated, and hope that you concur. The updated tRNAdb is reachable at https://tdb.bioinf.uni-leipzig.de/ and is still undergoing minor revision.
